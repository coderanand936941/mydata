Privacy Policy

This starter privacy page should be replaced with a policy tailored to your business and reviewed before launch.

Information collected

Depending on enabled features, the platform may store name, email, profile details, project information, messages, partner assignment and account activity.

Passwords

Nexora should never store or display users' plaintext passwords. Authentication should be handled by Firebase Authentication.

Data access

Users should only be able to access data permitted by their account role and the platform's security rules.
